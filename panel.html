Enter your Spotify Web API Client ID. In the Spotify app dashboard, add http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback as a Redirect URI. After installing this version, authorize again once to grant playlist access.

Use {song}/{artist} for the outgoing track and {next_song}/{next_artist} for the queued track. Choose 0 seconds to cue PETEY right as the song ends. The event stays hidden and speech respects PETEY’s speaker mute button.

Fade Music While PETEY Speaks

PETEY captures the active Spotify device’s current volume, fades down when speech actually begins, and restores that exact volume when speech ends. Muted replies do not change Spotify.
